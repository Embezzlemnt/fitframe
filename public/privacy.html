Privacy, plainly.

what we collect

  • email address — used to notify you when your batch opens
  • face measurements — pupillary distance, bridge width, temple length, face height, face width, lens height. these are numbers, not images. computed on your device from the camera feed.
  • frame selection — your chosen frame style, colorway, and lens preference
  • timestamp — when you joined the waitlist

what we do not collect

no camera images. the camera feed runs in your browser only. no frame, photo, or video is transmitted or stored anywhere.
no account or password. fitframe runs in the browser. there is no login, dashboard, or customer profile.
no payment data. payment processing is handled by stripe. fitframe never sees your card details.

how we store it

data is stored in cloudflare kv, a globally distributed key-value store running on us-based edge infrastructure. we retain it until the order is fulfilled or you request deletion.

on biometric data

the measurements we collect (pd, bridge width, temple length, face height, face width, lens height) are functional fit parameters — the same numbers an optician writes on a prescription pad. they are not biometric identifiers used for recognition or identification. some states define facial measurements differently under biometric privacy laws. we describe our data accurately and recommend you consult your own legal counsel if you have concerns.

your rights

you can request deletion of your data at any time. email hello@fitframe.store with the email address you used to join the waitlist and we will remove your entry within 14 days.

third parties

we use cloudflare for hosting and edge storage, resend for transactional email, and stripe for payment processing. each operates under its own privacy and data processing agreement. we do not sell, share, or monetize your data.

changes

if this policy changes materially, we will update this page and note the change in our next waitlist email. last updated: may 2026.
