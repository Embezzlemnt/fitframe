Privacy, plainly.

FitFrame uses your camera only to measure frame fit. The camera feed runs in your browser during the scan.

No face images are stored. The scan produces fit measurements. It does not save photos or video.
No face images are transmitted. The order email includes measurements, frame choice, style answers, and shipping address.
No app account is required. FitFrame runs in the browser. There is no login, password, or customer dashboard.

Shipping details are used to make and deliver the order. FitFrame does not sell customer contact or shipping information.
