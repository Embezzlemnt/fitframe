privacy, plainly.

what we collect

  • your email — so we can reach you when your batch opens
  • your face measurements — pd, bridge, temple, face width, face height, lens height. numbers only, never images. measured on your device, then submitted to us when you join the waitlist or send your fit details so we can build your frame.
  • your frame choice — style, colorway, lens preference

what we don't collect

no camera images. your camera runs in your browser. no photo, frame, or video reaches us.
no account. no login, no dashboard, no password.
no payment details. we never see your card. payment is handled securely at checkout.

how it's stored

when you submit the waitlist form, your email, measurement numbers, and frame choice are stored on our infrastructure. those same details are included in the emails we send — one to you, one to us — for fulfillment and review. we keep this data until your order ships or you ask us to delete it.

your measurements

the numbers from your scan — pupillary distance, bridge width, temple length, face width, face height, lens height — are approximate structural fit parameters. they are calculated in your browser using MediaPipe, a facial landmark tool from Google Research. they help us size your frame, but they are not a clinical eye exam. these numbers are not used for recognition or stored as images, and they are only shared with the service providers listed below.

your rights

email us at hello@fitframe.store from the address you used to join. we'll remove your data within 14 days, no questions.

third parties

we work with a small number of trusted providers to run the site, send order emails, and handle payment. none of them receive more than what's needed. we don't sell your data.

changes

if this policy changes materially, we'll update this page and include a note in the next waitlist email.
