what lenses come with it?
every founding pair ships with blue light lenses, included in the $119. transition and prescription lenses are on the roadmap and will open after launch.
can I get prescription lenses?
not on the first batch — it ships non-prescription with blue light included. prescription support is coming. join the list and we'll tell you the moment it opens.
how accurate is the scan?
the scan uses a facial landmark system (MediaPipe) plus an iris or credit-card reference to set a real millimeter scale. it captures the same fit parameters an optician measures — pupillary distance, bridge, temple, lens height, face width — with a target of 1–2mm for frame fit.
what if they don't fit?
every order includes one free reprint. if the first pair isn't right, tell us what's off and we'll remake it. no shipping it back and forth — it's printed to order, so we just print it again.
what are the frames made of?
carbon-fiber nylon — light, durable, and dimensionally precise. each frame is printed to your measurements, hand-finished, and shipped from America.
when do I order, and when does it ship?
nothing is made until you order it. we're in final production on the first batch — join the list and we'll reach out when yours can be built. from there it's printed and shipped from America in about ten days.
is this legit?
yes. it's a small US operation, and every order is fulfilled by the person who built it. the official domain is fitframe.store.
