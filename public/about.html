about

it started with a problem I couldn't shop my way out of. I stopped wearing glasses because none of them fit — too wide, sliding down, pinching at the temples. every frame was built for a face that wasn't mine. turns out that's most people. glasses are made for an "average" face that doesn't really exist, and everyone else just settles.

FitFrame is the alternative. one scan maps your face in real millimeters, and a frame gets printed to those exact numbers — built for your geometry, not adjusted to it. everything is made in America, only after you order, so nothing sits in a warehouse and nothing goes to waste. no app, no store, no optician. just a frame that fits because it was built for you.

we're early, and we're building this carefully. we don't make claims we can't back, we don't hide how it's made, and we're not pretending to be bigger than we are. it's a small operation with one obsession: frames that fit real faces. if you've ever given up on glasses, this one was made for you.
