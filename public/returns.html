returns & reprints

every pair is printed to your exact measurements, so there's no shelf to put a return back on — and no off-the-rack pair to swap it for. instead of returns, we stand behind the fit.

the reprint guarantee

if your first pair doesn't fit right, we reprint it once, free. tell us what's off — too wide, too tight, sliding — and we'll remake it. nothing to ship back and forth; it's made to order, so we just build it again.

damaged on arrival

if your frame shows up damaged or with a manufacturing defect, we replace it at no cost. send a photo and we'll get a new one printed right away.

how to reach us

email hello@fitframe.store from the address you ordered with, within 30 days of delivery. one reprint per order. a real person reads every message.
