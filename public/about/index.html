About FitFrame

FitFrame is a made-to-measure eyewear company based in Pennsylvania.

Lorenzo founded FitFrame at 19 and currently fulfills every order personally.

The product uses a browser-based face scan and PA12 nylon 3D printing to make custom-fit frames.

Lorenzo has a sales background and builds the product himself.
