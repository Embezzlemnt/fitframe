# Future Features

## The Archive
Every pair that ships gets added to a running public record.
Pair 001, Pair 047, Pair 113.
Different every time. Living proof that no two are the same.
Display order numbers as part of the company narrative.

## Customer Profile Digest
After scan completes, generate a personalized profile that
visualizes the user's facial geometry without exact measurements.
Abstract visualization of their face proportions - provocative
think-tank feature, not vanity metric. No hints toward
attractive/unattractive language. Take away or keep mm visibility
as a design decision later.

Both features wait until core UX is launched and validated.
